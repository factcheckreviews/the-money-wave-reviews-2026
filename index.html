What is The Money Wave?

The Money Wave is a 7-minute audio track that uses layered theta-frequency sound to help listeners enter a calm, focused mental state. It’s designed to be used once daily with headphones, while seated or lying down, no meditation experience required.

The program is based on the general principle of brainwave entrainment — the idea that listening to rhythmic audio at specific frequencies can encourage the brain to sync with those rhythms. Theta waves (roughly 4–8 Hz) are commonly associated with relaxed alertness, creativity, and light meditative states.

Unlike manifestation courses with hours of video lessons, The Money Wave is just the audio file, plus a short quick-start PDF. You press play, listen for 7 minutes, and you’re done.
